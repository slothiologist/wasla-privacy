Wasla — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE] · Applies to the Wasla iOS app

Wasla helps you find an EV charger you can actually pull into and use, in the UAE. To do that it needs to know where you are. This page explains exactly what that means in practice, what leaves your phone, and what doesn't.

The short version.

  • You don't make an account. There's no name, email or phone number unless you choose to sign in with Apple.
  • Your location is used to search for chargers near you. We don't keep a record of where you've been.
  • When you check in at a charger, we save which charger — never your coordinates.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and there is no advertising or analytics tracking in the app.
  • You can ask us to delete everything, and we will.

Location — the important one

What we ask for

Wasla asks for location permission in two steps, and only when the feature that needs it is actually used:

  • While using the app. To find the nearest chargers you can use. Without this, the app can't do its main job.
  • Always (optional). Only if you want automatic check-ins — Wasla notices you've arrived at a charger and offers to mark it busy for other drivers. You can decline this and everything else keeps working.

What actually happens to it

This is the part most policies gloss over, so here is the mechanism:

  • Searching. Your coordinates are sent to our database to run the search — that is the only way to work out which chargers are near you. They are used to compute the results and are not written to any table. We do not build a location history from searches.
  • Arriving at a charger. The detection happens on your phone. iOS tells the app you've entered a small zone around a charger; your phone decides that, not our server. If you confirm a check-in, what we store is which charger, and that it was a verified arrival — a charger ID and a true/false flag. Your GPS coordinates are never uploaded or stored for this.
  • Navigation. When you tap Navigate, Wasla hands the charger's coordinates to your chosen maps app (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze). From that point you're in their app, under their privacy policy. Wasla doesn't follow the drive.

What we don't do: no background location trail, no advertising identifiers, no location sold or shared with data brokers, no profiling of your movements. Location is never used for advertising, because there is no advertising.

Signing in (or not)

Anonymous by default

On first launch Wasla creates an anonymous session. You get a random identifier (a UUID) and nothing else — no name, email, phone number or password is requested or held. That identifier is what your saved car and contributions attach to.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

You can optionally upgrade your anonymous session to a permanent one using Sign in with Apple, so your data survives a reinstall or moves to a new device. If you do:

  • We receive an email address from Apple. If you chose “Hide My Email”, that is Apple's private relay address and we never see your real one.
  • Apple provides your name once, at first sign-in only. We store it as a display name.
  • We never receive your Apple ID password. We do not ask for or store passwords at all.

Sign-in is entirely optional. The core of the app — finding chargers, the map, occupancy, ratings, prices, navigation and one saved car — works without it, free and with no account.

Free-text you write

Parking-level notes (“Level 2, near the lifts”) are free text you type. Please don't put personal information in them. These notes are readable only by you; what other drivers see is the resolved directions text on the charger, without any author attached.

One thing you should know about community data

Occupancy check-ins, ratings, access votes and fault reports are publicly readable, including the anonymous ID that made them. That ID carries no name or contact details.

But we'd rather be straight with you than technically accurate: because those rows carry a charger and a timestamp, someone could in principle look at a single anonymous ID and infer “this ID charged at these places at these times”. That is not your identity, but it is a pattern. We're telling you because we think you should be able to decide what you contribute knowing that. If you sign in with Apple, that pattern becomes linked to an account, and we re-examine this before every release that touches it.

There are no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs in Wasla. No Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no advertising identifier, no cross-app tracking. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

Where your data is kept

Our database is hosted by Supabase on Amazon Web Services in the Singapore region (ap-southeast-1). This means your personal data is transferred outside the UAE.

Under Articles 22 and 23 of the PDPL, we rely on contractual safeguards with our processor requiring an adequate level of protection, and on your consent to use the app on that basis. Apple and Google also process data on their own global infrastructure under their respective terms.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you use Wasla. There is currently no automatic expiry — we would rather tell you that than quote a retention period we don't actually enforce.

  • Ask us to delete and we remove your profile, vehicles, sessions, favourites, trips and contributions.
  • Delete the app without asking us and the data stays on our server, because an anonymous session can't tell us you've gone. Worse, deleting the app also removes your data ID from the phone — so send the deletion request before you uninstall, or we may not be able to find your records.
  • Purchase records may be kept longer where we're required to for tax, accounting or dispute purposes.
  • Aggregate charger statistics (how busy a charger tends to be) are not linked to you and remain after deletion.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Privacy → Request data deletion. That opens an email with your data ID already filled in — just send it. We'll confirm within 30 days as the PDPL requires, and usually much sooner.

You can also email WaslaSupport@proton.me directly. Include the ID shown under Settings → Privacy → Your data ID (tap it to copy).

Because Wasla is designed to work without an account, that ID is normally the only thing that identifies your records — we deliberately hold no name, email or phone number to look you up by. If you signed in with Apple, the linked email works too.

Your rights under the PDPL

You have the right to:

  • Know what we hold about you and get a copy of it
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete your data (the right to erasure)
  • Restrict or object to how we process it
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format. Your charging history can already be exported as a CSV from within the app.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including by turning off location permission in iOS Settings. The app will keep working, with less accuracy.
  • Complain to the UAE Data Office if you think we've got it wrong

To exercise any of these, email WaslaSupport@proton.me. We don't charge for this and we won't degrade your experience for asking.

What we base this on

  • Your consent — for location access and for optional sign-in.
  • Performing our agreement with you — saving your car, your preferences and your Pro unlock.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping community data trustworthy and preventing abuse, balanced against your privacy.

Automated decisions

Wasla scores chargers automatically — how confident we are that a charger is publicly accessible, and how reliable it has been. These decisions are about chargers, not about you, and have no legal or similarly significant effect on you.

We do weight community contributions by a trust score derived from your past contributions. This affects how much a report influences a charger's rating. It does not restrict your use of the app, and you can ask us what your score is.

Security

Every table is protected by row-level security so one user cannot read or change another's private records. The app holds only a public, restricted key — the privileged key never ships inside it. Purchases are verified on our server against Apple, never trusted from the app. We review this posture on every change that touches permissions.

No system is perfect. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will notify you and the UAE Data Office as the PDPL requires.

Children

Wasla is for licensed drivers and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided data, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. For anything significant we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
